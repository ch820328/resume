# Google Interview Strengthening Rules (AI Instruction)
# Google 面試強化規則 (AI 指引)

These rules are derived from HR feedback and Google Leadership Principles to ensure behavioral answers meet the "Google Readiness" standard for L4/Senior roles.
這些規則源自 HR 反饋與 Google 領導力原則，旨在確保行為面試回答符合 L4/Senior 職位的 "Google Readiness" 標準。

---

## 1. Narrative Coherence (敘事連貫性)
- **Rule (規則):** Do NOT simply list technical facts, layers, or tasks (Avoid the "Tier and Layer" issue).
- **說明 (Explanation):** 避免單純堆疊技術事實或層級。
- **Instruction (指引):** Connect every action into a single coherent story. Show how one step led to the next logically. Use transition phrases like "Consequently," "This led me to," or "To mitigate the risk of..."
- **中文指引:** 將每個行動串連成一個完整連貫的故事。展示步驟之間的邏輯演進，使用轉折詞如「因此」、「這引導我去做...」或「為了降低 X 風險，我決定...」。

## 2. Decision Rationale & Google Cognitive Ability (決策邏輯與認知能力)
- **Rule (規則):** Every action must be justified by a "Why" (Decision-making rationale).
- **說明 (Explanation):** 每個行動都必須有「為什麼」來支撐（展現決策邏輯）。
- **Instruction (指引):** Show your "Cognitive Ability" by explaining the data, metrics, or trade-offs behind your decisions. Do not just say *what* you did; explain the logical evolution of your thinking.
- **中文指引:** 透過解釋決策背後的數據、指標或權衡來展示你的「認知能力」。不要只說你「做了什麼」，要解釋你思考過程的邏輯演變。

## 3. Impact & Ambiguity (影響力與應對模糊性)
- **Rule (規則):** Prioritize Impact and Ambiguity over simple implementation.
- **說明 (Explanation):** 優先強調影響力與在模糊情況下的決策，而非單純的執行細節。
- **Instruction (指引):** Highlight how you made progress when requirements were unclear (**Comfort with Ambiguity**). Quantify the "Result" with data (e.g., % improvement) to show business impact.
- **中文指引:** 強調你在需求不明確時如何推進工作（**應對模糊性的能力**）。用數據（如百分比提升）來量化「結果」，以展示對業務的影響。

## 4. Leadership & Googleyness (領導力與 Google 特質)
- **Rule (規則):** Demonstrate "Leadership without Authority" and "Care for the Team."
- **說明 (Explanation):** 展現「無職權領導力」與「對團隊的關懷」。
- **Instruction (指引):** Show you are a **Force Multiplier**. Highlight actions where you empowered others, sought feedback proactively (**Pre-Mid-Post project feedback loops**), or mentored peers to help the team grow (**Intellectual Humility**).
- **中文指引:** 展示你是團隊的「戰力倍增器」。強調你賦能他人、主動尋求反饋（**項目前、中、後的反饋環節**）或指導同事以幫助團隊成長的行動（**展現謙遜與學習心態**）。

## 5. STAR-L / SPSIL Framework (答題架構)
- **Rule (規則):** All stories must follow Situation, Task, Action, Result, and Learning/Leadership.
- **說明 (Explanation):** 所有故事必須遵循 STAR-L (SPSIL) 架構。
- **Instruction (指引):** 
    - **Action (60%):** Must be the bulk of the answer. Focus on "I," not "We."
    - **Result:** Quantifiable data.
    - **Learning/Leadership:** A closing hook that demonstrates your maturity and Googleyness.
- **中文指引:** 
    - **行動 (佔 60%):** 回答的核心。強調「我」做了什麼，而非「我們」。
    - **結果:** 可量化的數據。
    - **學習與領導力:** 結尾需展示你的成熟度與 Google 特質。

---
**Usage:** AI should read these rules before reviewing or generating any behavioral interview stories for this user.
**用途:** AI 在為使用者審閱或生成行為面試故事前，應先讀取這些規則。
