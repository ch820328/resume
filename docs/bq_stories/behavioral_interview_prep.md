# Google/Amazon Behavioral Interview Preparation Guide
# Google/Amazon 行為面試準備手冊

This document serves as a comprehensive master list for preparing Behavioral Questions (BQ) for Google and Amazon interviews. It integrates Google's core evaluation dimensions (Googleyness, Leadership, GCA) with Amazon's Leadership Principles.
本文件為 Google 與 Amazon 面試行為問題 (BQ) 的完整準備清單。它整合了 Google 的核心評鑑維度（Googleyness, Leadership, GCA）與 Amazon 的領導力準則 (Leadership Principles)。

---

## 🛠 The STAR Method
## 🛠 STAR 回答法

For every story, follow this structure:
每個故事請遵循以下架構：

*   **S (Situation):** Set the scene. (10%)
    **S (情境):** 交代背景。(10%)
*   **T (Task):** What was the challenge or goal? (10%)
    **T (任務):** 當時面臨的挑戰或目標。(10%)
*   **A (Action):** What did **YOU** specifically do? How did you think? (60%)
    **A (行動):** 你具體做了什麼？你的思考邏輯為何？(60%)
*   **R (Result):** What was the outcome? Use metrics and reflections. (20%)
    **R (結果):** 最終結果為何？請使用量化數據與反思。(20%)

## 📋 Master Story Checklist
## 📋 萬用故事清單

### Category 1: Googleyness & Ambiguity
### 第一類：文化契合度與處理模糊

*Focus: Cultural fit, navigating uncertainty, and being helpful.*
*核心：文化契合度、在不確定性中前進、主動協助他人。*

- [ ] **1. Dealing with Ambiguity**
      **1. 處理模糊**
      A time when project goals were unclear or shifted mid-way.
      描述一次專案目標不明確或中途大改的經驗。
- [ ] **2. Helpfulness / Team First**
      **2. 團隊精神**
      A time you went out of your way to help a colleague or share knowledge.
      主動協助同事或分享知識的經驗（非職責要求）。
- [ ] **3. User First**
      **3. 用戶至上**
      A time you advocated for the user's experience over technical ease.
      為了提升用戶體驗而選擇較困難技術路徑的例子。
- [ ] **4. Learning & Curiosity**
      **4. 學習熱情**
      A recent technology you learned. How did you master it quickly?
      最近學到的新技術，以及你如何快速掌握並應用它。

### Category 2: Leadership & Influence
### 第二類：領導力與影響力

*Focus: Ownership and influencing without formal authority.*
*核心：主人翁精神、發揮非職權影響力。*

- [ ] **5. Ownership**
      **5. 主人翁精神**
      A time you spotted a system flaw and took the initiative to fix it.
      發現系統漏洞或流程缺陷並主動解決的經驗。
- [ ] **6. Influencing Others**
      **6. 說服他人**
      A time you persuaded a senior peer or lead to adopt your proposal.
      成功說服資深同事或主管接受你技術方案的經歷。
- [ ] **7. Mentorship**
      **7. 導師精神**
      A time you mentored a junior member or led a small group to success.
      指導新人或帶領小組達成目標的經驗。
- [ ] **8. Decision Making**
      **8. 決策能力**
      A time you chose between two equally good technical options.
      在兩個同等優秀的技術方案中做出抉擇的邏輯。

### Category 3: General Cognitive Ability (GCA)
### 第三類：綜合認知能力

*Focus: Problem-solving logic and technical depth.*
*核心：解決問題的邏輯與技術深度。*

- [ ] **9. Complex Problem Solving**
      **9. 解決複雜問題**
      The hardest technical bug or bottleneck you've solved.
      你解決過最難的 Bug 或效能瓶頸，請解釋診斷邏輯。
- [ ] **10. Data-Driven Decisions**
      **10. 數據導向**
      A time you used data to optimize a system or disprove a wrong intuition.
      利用數據優化系統或推翻錯誤直覺的例子。
- [ ] **11. Invent & Simplify**
      **11. 創新與簡化**
      A time you automated a complex manual process.
      將複雜手動流程自動化以節省成本或時間。
- [ ] **12. High Standards**
      **12. 追求卓越**
      A time you refused to compromise on quality despite pressure.
      在壓力下仍堅持程式碼品質或測試覆蓋率的經驗。

### Category 4: Conflict & Teamwork
### 第四類：衝突處理與團隊協作

*Focus: Emotional intelligence and handling disagreements.*
*核心：情商與如何處理意見分歧。*

- [ ] **13. Disagreement**
      **13. 意見分歧**
      A time you had a major technical disagreement with a peer.
      與同事產生重大技術分歧時，如何專業地解決？
- [ ] **14. Receiving Feedback**
      **14. 接受回饋**
      A time you received negative feedback or a challenge.
      收到負面回饋或挑戰時，你的反應與改進措施。
- [ ] **15. Stakeholder Management**
      **15. 利害關係人管理**
      Managing unrealistic expectations from a PM.
      如何管理 PM 或其他部門不合理的預期。
- [ ] **16. Team Collaboration**
      **16. 團隊協作**
      Helping a demoralized or struggling team get back on track.
      幫助士氣低落或進度落後的團隊重回正軌。

### Category 5: Failure & Growth
### 第五類：失敗與成長

*Focus: Self-awareness and the ability to learn from mistakes.*
*核心：自我覺察與從失敗中學習的能力。*

- [ ] **17. Admit a Mistake**
      **17. 承認錯誤**
      A time you made a significant technical error.
      犯下重大技術錯誤的經歷，以及你如何止血與預防。
- [ ] **18. Missed Deadline**
      **18. 错过期限**
      A time you couldn't deliver on time. How did you communicate?
      無法準時交付時的溝通方式與教訓。
- [ ] **19. Reflection**
      **19. 自我反思**
      What would you do differently today with your current knowledge?
      回頭看舊專案，以現在的知識你會如何改進？
