# 03. User First | 用戶至上
## Project: Baby Tracker (Family Collaboration Hub)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    Caring for a newborn involves a complex ecosystem of caregivers—parents, grandparents, and babysitters. In my family, we were struggling with fragmented information. We relied on verbal updates or chaotic instant messaging, which led to "Communication Friction." For example, one person wouldn't know if the baby had already been fed or when the next medication was due, leading to high stress and safety concerns.
*   **Task**: 
    My goal was to create a **Shared Source of Truth** that put the family's collaborative needs first, ensuring seamless information flow without constant verbal checking.
*   **Action**: 
    I developed the **Baby Tracker App** with a focus on **Real-time Ecosystem Sync**. I implemented a shared dashboard where every activity (feeding, sleep, health) is synced instantly across devices. Beyond just logging, I integrated a **Daily Planning Module** so caregivers could see upcoming tasks and schedules. I prioritized a "Zero-Friction" UX with large, one-handed buttons, recognizing that caregivers often have only one hand free.
*   **Result**: 
    Completely eliminated the "verbal status checking" overhead. Family communication became 100% data-driven, reducing stress and ensuring no missed feedings or double-dosages of medication. The app became the central planning hub for the entire family.
*   **Learning**: 
    "User First" isn't just about the individual's button clicks; it's about understanding the **Social Context** of the user. By solving the communication gap between users, you create a tool that has much higher emotional and functional value.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    照護新生兒涉及多位照顧者——父母、長輩與保姆。在我的家庭中，我們正面臨資訊碎片化的問題。我們依賴口頭更新或混亂的通訊軟體訊息，這導致了嚴重的「溝通摩擦」。例如，有人不知道嬰兒是否已經餵過奶，或者下次用藥是什麼時候，這導致了巨大的壓力與安全隱憂。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    我的目標是建立一個以家庭協作需求為優先的 **「共享事實來源 (Shared Source of Truth)」**，確保資訊能無縫流動，而無需頻繁地口頭確認。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我開發了 **Baby Tracker App**，專注於 **「家庭生態系實時同步」**。我實作了一個共享儀表板，讓每項活動（餵食、睡眠、健康）都能即時同步到所有裝置。除了記錄之外，我還整合了 **「日常計畫模組」**，讓照顧者能看到未來的任務與行程。我優先考慮了「零摩擦」的 UX 設計，採用大面積的單手操作按鈕，因為照顧者通常只有一隻手是有空的。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    徹底消除了「口頭狀態確認」的開銷。家庭溝通變成了 100% 數據導向，減輕了壓力並確保不會遺漏餵食或重複用藥。該 App 成為了全家人的核心計畫中心。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    「用戶至上」不僅僅是關於個人的按鈕點擊，而是關於理解用戶的 **「社會情境 (Social Context)」**。透過解決用戶間的溝通差，你創造出的工具將具有更高的情感與功能價值。

### ❓ 模擬問答 (Possible Q&A)

1.  **問：「你是如何定義這個 App 的『用戶』的？」**
    *   **🇺🇸 English**: "I defined the user not as a 'person,' but as a **'Caregiving Unit.'** This mindset shifted the design from a personal diary to a collaborative tool. It meant that features like 'Conflict Resolution' and 'Instant Notification' became higher priority than fancy data visualizations."
    *   **🇹🇼 中文**: 「我將用戶定義為一個 **『照護單位』** 而非個人。這種心態將設計從個人日記轉向了協作工具。這意味著像是『衝突解決』和『即時通知』的功能，優先級比花哨的數據視覺化更高。」

2.  **問：「在設計『計畫規劃』功能時，你如何平衡靈活性與規範性？」**
    *   **🇺🇸 English**: "Baby schedules are unpredictable. I designed the planning module to be **'Suggestive, not Restrictive.'** It provides a baseline schedule based on previous data, but allows any family member to override or adjust it instantly. This flexibility was key to making the tool helpful rather than annoying during a busy day."
    *   **🇹🇼 中文**: 「嬰兒的作息是不可預測的。我將計畫模組設計為 **『建議性而非限制性』**。它根據歷史數據提供基礎行程，但允許任何家庭成員即時覆蓋或調整。這種靈活性是讓工具變得有用而非干擾的關鍵。」

3.  **問：「評分 (Score)」**
    *   **Rating**: **8.5/10** (展現了強大的產品思維與對多人協作場景的深刻理解，這在設計複雜系統時是非常寶貴的特質。)
