# 06. Influencing Others | 說服他人
## Project: Central Dashboard (Driving Efficiency & Scaling Adoption)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    Our engineers were spending roughly 20% of their time on repetitive tasks like manually retrieving logs and switching between fragmented tools (Redmine, GitLab, etc.). I wanted to build a centralized "Engineering Hub" to automate these workflows, but it was initially viewed as an optional internal project.
*   **Task**: 
    Persuade senior management to prioritize this infrastructure project by demonstrating the massive gain in **Engineering Capacity**.
*   **Action**: 
    I collected **"Time Fragmentation" data**. I interviewed 10 engineers and quantified the total hours lost each month due to context switching and manual log retrieval. Using this data, I built a **"Functional Prototype"** that demonstrated a **300x speed improvement** (finding logs in 3 seconds instead of 15 minutes). I presented a clear efficiency report showing that the tool would recover over **200 engineering hours per month**, allowing teams to focus on critical product features instead of tool-related overhead.
*   **Result**: 
    Management immediately recognized the potential for improved development velocity. They approved the project and expanded the team to complete the full-scale deployment, which eventually reduced tool-related overhead by 60% across the department.
*   **Learning**: 
    To influence management, you must **visualize the "Invisible Waste."** By translating technical pain into **Recovered Capacity**, you make it clear that investing in internal tools is a strategic move to accelerate overall R&D velocity.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    我發現我們的工程師大約有 20% 的時間浪費在重複性任務上，例如手動檢索日誌以及在碎片化的工具（Redmine, GitLab 等）之間切換。我想建立一個中央化的「工程入口網」來自動化這些流程，但起初這被視為一個可有可無的內部專案。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    透過展示 **「研發產能 (Engineering Capacity)」** 的巨大增益，說服高層管理人員優先執行這項基礎設施專案。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我收集了 **「工時碎片化」數據**。我訪談了 10 位工程師，量化了每個月因上下文切換和手動檢索日誌而損失的總工時。利用這些數據，我製作了一個 **「功能原型」**，展示了 **300 倍的速度提升**（從 15 分鐘縮短至 3 秒內找到日誌）。我提交了一份清晰的效率報告，顯示該工具每個月將回收超過 **200 個工程師工時**，讓團隊能專注於核心產品功能，而非工具操作的開銷。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    管理層立即意識到這能顯著提升開發速度。他們批准了專案，並擴大了團隊規模以完成全面部署。最終，該工具將全體部門的工具相關開銷降低了 60%。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    要影響管理層，你必須 **將「隱形浪費」視覺化**。透過將技術痛苦轉化為 **「回收產能」**，你能讓主管清楚意識到：投資內部工具是加速整體研發速度的戰略舉措。
