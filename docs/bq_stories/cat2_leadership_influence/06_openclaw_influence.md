# 06. Influence / Leadership | 影響力
## Project: OpenClaw (Organic Scaling & Department-wide Adoption)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    OpenClaw MR was initially developed as an internal tool to solve code review bottlenecks within my immediate team. Its effectiveness in catching logic errors and saving time quickly became evident.
*   **Task**: 
    As the tool matured, I saw the potential to scale its benefits across the entire department and other engineering teams.
*   **Action**: 
    I focused on making the tool **"Self-Service and High-ROI."** I ensured the onboarding process was seamless and the feedback quality was high enough that it spoke for itself. Instead of "pushing" the tool, I relied on organic advocacy. I presented our team's improved review metrics—such as a **40% reduction in manual review time**—during department syncs. 
*   **Result**: 
    The tool's reputation spread quickly. We successfully expanded from a single team to **department-wide adoption**. Most importantly, **Project Owners from other teams** have started proactively inquiring about how to integrate OpenClaw into their own workflows. It has evolved from a local script into a cross-team engineering standard.
*   **Learning**: 
    Leadership is about **Building Value that Pulls Others In**. When you solve a universal pain point (like slow code reviews) with a high-quality solution, people will naturally seek you out. Influence is most effective when it's earned through demonstrated technical results.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    OpenClaw MR 最初是作為一個內部工具開發的，目的是為了解決我所在團隊內部的代碼審核瓶頸。它在捕捉邏輯錯誤和節省時間方面的成效迅速顯現。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    隨著工具趨於成熟，我看到了將其優點推廣到整個部門及其他工程團隊的潛力。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我專注於將工具打造為 **「可視化且高專業度」** 的方案。我開發了 **多維度品質矩陣**，將代碼質量量化為 7 個維度（邏輯、安全、性能等）的「品質雷達圖」。在部門同步會議中，我不僅分享數據，還展示了這個雷達圖如何直觀地揭示代碼中的 **知識缺口 (Knowledge Gaps)**。這種高度專業且透明的呈現方式，消除了團隊對 AI 審核「模糊不清」的疑慮。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    這種數據驅動且具備直觀視覺效果的工具迅速贏得了信任。我們成功從單一團隊擴展到 **全部門採用**。其他團隊的 **Project Owner** 被這種能全方位量化品質的能力所吸引，主動前來諮詢如何整合 OpenClaw 以提升他們的發佈門禁標準。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    領導力在於 **「將複雜的專業價值簡約化、可視化」**。透過將抽象的代碼品質轉化為直觀的 7 維度雷達圖，我成功降低了跨團隊溝通的門檻，讓技術優勢轉化為組織共識。

### ❓ 模擬問答 (Possible Q&A)

1.  **問：「為什麼其他團隊的 Project Owner 會對你的工具感興趣？你覺得核心吸引力是什麼？」(Value Proposition)**
    *   **🇺🇸 English**: "The core attraction is **Efficiency and Consistency**. Project Owners are always looking for ways to speed up their release cycles without sacrificing quality. When they saw my team shipping code 40% faster while still catching critical bugs through OpenClaw, the data spoke louder than any presentation could."
    *   **🇹🇼 中文**: 「核心吸引力在於 **『效率與一致性』**。Project Owner 總是在尋找能在不犧牲品質的情況下加快發佈週期的方法。當他們看到我的團隊在透過 OpenClaw 抓出關鍵 Bug 的同時，發佈速度還提升了 40%，數據本身就比任何簡報都更有說服力。」

2.  **問：「在推廣到全部門的過程中，你遇到了什麼挑戰？你如何處理不同團隊的反饋？」(Handling Objections)**
    *   **🇺🇸 English**: "The main challenge was handling diverse coding styles and project-specific rules. I implemented a **Modular Configuration** system, allowing different teams to toggle specific review agents or inject their own 'LLM Wiki' rules. By making the tool flexible, I removed the friction of adoption and earned their trust."
    *   **🇹🇼 中文**: 「主要挑戰是處理多樣的代碼風格與專案特定的規則。我實作了 **模組化配置系統**，讓不同的團隊能開關特定的審核 Agent，或注入他們自己的『LLM Wiki』規則。透過讓工具變得靈活，我移除了採用過程中的摩擦並贏得了他們的信任。」
