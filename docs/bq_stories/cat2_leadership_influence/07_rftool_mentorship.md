# 07. Mentorship | 導師精神
## Project: RFTool (Leading Team-Wide Framework Transition)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    After I enhanced RFTool with **Listeners** and **Built-in libraries**, the legacy standalone scripts became obsolete. However, the entire team was still following the old "Reporting Tool" mindset, leading to inconsistent test reliability across different projects.
*   **Task**: 
    Lead the **Entire Engineering Team** to transition to the new Full-Lifecycle Automation standards to ensure unified testing quality.
*   **Action**: 
    I conducted a **Comprehensive Technical Sharing Session** for the whole team. I didn't just show them the code; I explained the **Logic Shift**—from manual cleanup to using **Robot Framework Built-ins** and **Listeners** for automated lifecycle management. I established clear documentation and standardized templates that allowed every engineer to see how to migrate their scripts. I acted as the primary point of contact, answering questions and performing code reviews to ensure everyone was aligned with the new "Framework-First" approach.
*   **Result**: 
    The whole team successfully transitioned to the new standards within one month. This unified approach led to a **50% improvement** in test maintenance efficiency and established a consistent hardware testing protocol across the entire department.
*   **Learning**: 
    Mentorship on a team level is about **"Setting the North Star."** By providing a clear vision, unified standards, and the technical support to get there, you can move an entire organization toward a higher engineering standard simultaneously.

---

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    在我使用 **Listeners** 和 **Built-in 函式庫** 強化 RFTool 後，舊有的獨立腳本模式已顯得過時。然而，整個團隊仍停留在「僅看報告」的舊思維中，導致不同專案間的測試可靠性不一。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    帶領 **整個工程團隊** 轉向新的全生命週期自動化標準，以確保統一的測試品質。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我為全團隊舉行了一次 **全面的技術分享會**。我不僅僅是展示代碼，我還解釋了 **「邏輯轉變」**——從手動清理轉向使用 **Robot Framework Built-ins** 與 **Listeners** 進行自動化生命週期管理。我建立了清晰的文檔與標準化模板，讓每位工程師都能清楚如何遷移他們的腳本。我擔任主要的技術窗口，解答疑問並進行 Code Review，確保每個人都對齊了新的「框架優先 (Framework-First)」方法。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    全團隊在一個月內成功轉向新標準。這種統一的方法讓測試維護效率提升了 **50%**，並在全體部門中建立了一致的硬體測試協議。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    團隊層級的導師精神在於 **「設定北極星」**。透過提供清晰的願景、統一的標準以及達成目標所需的技術支援，你可以帶領整個組織同時邁向更高的工程標準。
