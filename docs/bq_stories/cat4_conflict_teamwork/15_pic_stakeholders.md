# 15. Stakeholder Management | 利害關係人管理 (Variant 3)
## Project: PIC Transcript (Managing Accuracy Expectations)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    After demoing the prototype of PIC Transcript, the factory director was impressed but demanded **"100% guaranteed accuracy"** before it could be used on the global production line. As an engineer, I knew that in a noisy CV environment, "100% guarantee" is a dangerous promise.
*   **Task**: 
    Manage the high expectations of a non-technical director while ensuring we have a path to deployment.
*   **Action**: 
    I used a **"Risk Mitigation Framework"** to communicate. Instead of saying "I can't promise 100%," I showed him a chart of our **Error Distribution**. I explained that while the recognition was 99% accurate, I had built a **"Validation Safety Net"**—a secondary script that cross-references the OCR output with the BIOS version number from a different source. I proposed a **"Pilot Phase"** with human auditing for the first 1,000 units. By framing the conversation around "Managing Risk" rather than "Absolute Perfection," I gained his trust.
*   **Result**: 
    The director approved the pilot. We proved that the "Safety Net" caught all critical misreads, and we successfully transitioned to full automation within two months.
*   **Learning**: 
    Stakeholder management is about **managing trust through transparency**. When dealing with high-stakes requirements, show them your "Safety Net" and your "Validation Data" rather than just giving a yes/no answer.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    在展示了 PIC Transcript 的原型後，工廠廠長印象深刻，但他要求在進入全球生產線前必須有 **「100% 的準確率保證」**。身為工程師，我知道在充滿雜訊的 CV 環境中，「100% 保證」是一個危險的承諾。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    管理非技術主管的高預期，同時確保我們有路徑可以正式部署。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我使用了 **「風險緩釋框架 (Risk Mitigation Framework)」** 來進行溝通。我沒有直接說「我不能保證 100%」，而是向他展示了我們的 **「錯誤分佈圖」**。我解釋說，雖然辨識率是 99%，但我建立了一個 **「驗證安全網」**——一個會將 OCR 輸出與來自不同來源的 BIOS 版本號進行交叉比對的次要腳本。我提出了一個 **「試行階段 (Pilot Phase)」**，前 1,000 台設備會經過人工稽核。透過將對話聚焦於「管理風險」而非「絕對完美」，我贏得了他的信任。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    廠長核准了試行計畫。我們證明了「安全網」捕捉到了所有關鍵誤讀，並在兩個月內成功過渡到全面自動化。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    利害關係人管理是關於 **「透過透明度來管理信任」**。在處理高風險需求時，向他們展示你的「安全網」和「驗證數據」，而不是只給出是或否的答案。
