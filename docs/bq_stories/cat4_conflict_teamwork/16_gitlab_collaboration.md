# 16. Team Collaboration | 團隊協作
## Project: GitLab CD (Psychological Safety & Automation)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    Our infrastructure team was suffering from "Deployment Anxiety." Manually updating firmware across hundreds of nodes was a high-risk task that often led to late-night emergency fixes. The team was stressed, and collaboration was strained because everyone was afraid of "breaking the production line."
*   **Task**: 
    I wanted to build a culture of safety and collaboration by automating the most stressful part of our job.
*   **Action**: 
    I didn't just build the technical CD pipeline; I focused on the **human experience of deployment**. I implemented "Auto-Rollbacks" and "Health Probes" to provide a safety net. More importantly, I held "Lunch & Learn" sessions to walk the team through the safety mechanisms. I encouraged everyone to contribute to the "Smoke Test" suite, making deployment a **Shared Responsibility** rather than a solo high-wire act. I fostered an environment where "failing fast" was handled by the system, not by blaming individuals.
*   **Result**: 
    Deployment-related stress dropped significantly. The team began deploying 3x more frequently, and the collaborative atmosphere shifted from "finger-pointing" to "improving the pipeline."
*   **Learning**: 
    True collaboration thrives on **Psychological Safety**. By building technical systems that protect people from their own mistakes, you allow them to focus on innovation and working together instead of living in fear.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    我們的基礎設施團隊正承受著「部署焦慮」。手動更新數百個節點的韌體是一項高風險任務，經常導致深夜的緊急修復。團隊壓力很大，協作也變得很緊張，因為每個人都害怕「弄壞生產線」。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    我希望透過自動化工作中壓力最大的部分，來建立一種安全與協作的文化。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我不僅僅是構建了技術上的 CD 流水線，我更專注於 **「部署的人性體驗」**。我實作了「自動回滾」和「健康檢查」來提供安全網。更重要的是，我舉辦了「午餐學習會 (Lunch & Learn)」，帶領團隊了解這些安全機制。我鼓勵每個人都為「冒煙測試」套件做貢獻，將部署變成 **「共同責任」**，而不是個人的冒險行為。我營造了一個環境，讓「快速失敗」由系統處理，而不是責怪個人。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    與部署相關的壓力顯著下降。團隊部署頻率提升了 3 倍，協作氣氛也從「互相指責」轉向「共同改進流水線」。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    真正的協作建立在 **「心理安全感 (Psychological Safety)」** 之上。透過構建能保護人們免於犯錯的技術系統，你讓他們能專注於創新與合作，而不是生活在恐懼中。
