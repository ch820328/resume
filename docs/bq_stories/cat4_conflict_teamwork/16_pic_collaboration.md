# 16. Team Collaboration | 團隊協作 (Variant 3)
## Project: PIC Transcript (Optics & Software Sync)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    The "PIC Transcript" visual recognition tool was failing in certain factories due to inconsistent lighting on the production line. My software team was blaming the "Factory Environment," while the Hardware/Optics team was blaming our "Weak Algorithm."
*   **Task**: 
    Break the "Blame Culture" and lead a cross-functional collaboration to solve the root cause.
*   **Action**: 
    I organized a **"Joint Debugging Taskforce."** I brought a hardware engineer and an optics specialist into my lab. I showed them exactly how the image features "shifted" under different light temperatures. Instead of asking them to "fix the factory," I asked: "How can we create a **Standardized Lighting Profile** that our software can recognize?" We co-designed a low-cost LED shroud for the cameras that provided a consistent light environment. I then updated the software to include a **"Calibration Mode"** designed specifically for the hardware team to verify the setup.
*   **Result**: 
    Recognition accuracy reached 99.9% globally. The two teams stopped finger-pointing and started using our "Joint Taskforce" model for all future visual inspection projects.
*   **Learning**: 
    Collaboration isn't about "staying in your lane." It's about **reaching across the aisle** to understand the physical constraints of other teams and building "bridges" (like calibration tools) that help both teams succeed.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    「PIC Transcript」視覺辨識工具在某些工廠因生產線光影不一而失效。我的軟體團隊責怪「工廠環境」，而硬體/光學團隊則責怪我們的「演算法太弱」。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    打破「指責文化」，領導一次跨職能協作來解決根本原因。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我組織了一個 **「聯合調試小組 (Joint Debugging Taskforce)」**。我邀請了一位硬體工程師和一位光學專家來到我的實驗室。我向他們展示了影像特徵在不同色溫下是如何「偏移」的。我沒有要求他們「去修理工廠」，而是問：「我們如何建立一個軟體能識別的 **『標準化光影配置 (Lighting Profile)』**？」我們共同設計了一個低成本的 LED 遮光罩，為相機提供穩定的光影環境。接著我更新了軟體，加入了一個專門為硬體團隊設計的 **「校準模式 (Calibration Mode)」**，以便他們驗證設置。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    全球辨識準確率達到了 99.9%。兩個團隊不再互相推諉，並開始在所有未來的視覺檢測專案中沿用這種「聯合小組」模式。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    協作不是「各掃門前雪」。它是關於 **主動跨出領域** 去理解其他團隊的物理限制，並構建「橋樑」（如校準工具）來幫助雙方共同成功。
