# 18. Missed Deadline | 错过期限
## Project: Jetson BSP (Smart Build Integration)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    We were two weeks away from a major customer delivery of the Jetson BSP build system. However, we hit a major roadblock: the automated flash scripts were failing on a specific hardware SKU that we hadn't accounted for in our initial design.
*   **Task**: 
    I realized we would not be able to deliver the "Full Feature Set" by the original deadline without compromising on quality.
*   **Action**: 
    I took **proactive communication** as my primary strategy. Instead of working in silence and missing the date, I immediately informed the stakeholders. I presented a **Tiered Delivery Plan**: we would deliver the stable build for the primary SKU on time, and push the secondary SKU support by one week. I explained the technical bottleneck and showed that this was necessary to ensure manufacturing reliability. I spent the extra week pair-programming with the hardware team to solve the root cause.
*   **Result**: 
    The customer received the primary build on time and was impressed by our transparency. We delivered the second part a week later with zero bugs.
*   **Learning**: 
    Missing a deadline is a communication challenge, not just a technical one. Stakeholders value "No Surprises" and a "Plan B" more than a delayed, buggy release.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    距離向大客戶交付 Jetson BSP 建置系統僅剩兩週。然而，我們遇到了一個重大的障礙：自動化刷機腳本在一個我們最初設計時未考慮到的特定硬體 SKU 上失效了。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    我意識到在不犧牲品質的情況下，我們無法在原定限期前交付「完整功能集」。
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    我採取了 **「主動溝通」** 作為首要策略。我沒有悶著頭做然後最後放鴿子，而是立即通知了所有利害關係人。我提出了一套 **「分層交付計畫」**：我們將如期交付主要 SKU 的穩定版本，並將次要 SKU 的支援延後一週。我解釋了技術瓶頸，並說明這是為了確保生產可靠性所必需的。在多出來的那週裡，我與硬體團隊進行結對編程 (Pair-programming)，解決了根本原因。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    客戶如期收到了主要版本，並對我們的透明度印象深刻。一週後我們交付了第二部分，且零 Bug。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    错过期限是一個溝通挑戰，而不僅僅是技術挑戰。利害關係人更看重「沒有驚喜（預期內）」和「備案 (Plan B)」，而不是一個延遲且充滿 Bug 的發佈。
