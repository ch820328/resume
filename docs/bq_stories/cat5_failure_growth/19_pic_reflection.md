# 19. Reflection | 自我反思
## Project: PIC Transcript (Visual Recognition)

### 🎭 STAR Story (English)

*   **Situation**: 
    Two years ago, I built PIC Transcript using OpenCV template matching to solve our legacy hardware recognition problems. It was a successful project at the time, achieving 99% accuracy and speeding up BIOS validation by 4x.
*   **Task**: 
    Reflecting on that project today, what would I do differently with my current knowledge?
*   **Action**: 
    If I were to start that project today, I would move from "Deterministic Math" to a **Hybrid AI approach**. While template matching was efficient, it still required manual "anchor" creation for every new hardware model. With my current expertise in LLMs and Vision Models, I would implement an **Auto-Anchor Discovery** layer. I would use a lightweight Vision Transformer (ViT) to automatically identify regions of interest without manual labeling. This would transform a "highly efficient tool" into a "truly autonomous platform" that scales effortlessly to any new hardware SKU.
*   **Result**: 
    While the original tool saved 25 minutes per case, the "Reflected Version" would likely eliminate the initial 4-hour setup time for new hardware models entirely.
*   **Learning**: 
    A professional engineer's best skill is the ability to look at their own past success and see the "obsolescence." True growth comes from recognizing that the best solution of yesterday is just a stepping stone for the better solution of today.

### 🎭 STAR Story (中文)

*   **情境 (Situation)**: 
    兩年前，我使用 OpenCV 模板匹配建立了 PIC Transcript，解決了我們的舊硬體辨識問題。當時那是一個成功的專案，達成了 99% 的準確率並將 BIOS 驗證速度提升了 4 倍。
*   **任務 (Task)**: 
    以現在的知識回頭看那個專案，我會做出什麼不同的選擇？
*   **行動 (Action)**: 
    如果今天重新開始那個專案，我會從「確定性數學」轉向 **「混合 AI 方案」**。雖然模板匹配很高效，但它仍需要為每個新硬體型號手動建立「錨點」。憑藉我現在在 LLM 和視覺模型 (Vision Models) 方面的專業知識，我會實作一個 **「自動錨點發現層」**。我會使用輕量級的 Vision Transformer (ViT) 來自動識別感興趣區域，而無需手動標註。這將把一個「高效工具」轉化為一個能輕鬆擴展到任何新 SKU 的「真正自主平台」。
*   **結果 (Result)**: 
    原始工具為每個案例節省了 25 分鐘，而「反思後的版本」可能會完全消除新硬體型號最初的 4 小時設置時間。
*   **反思 (Learning)**: 
    專業工程師最好的技能是看著自己過去的成功並看到其中的「過時」。真正的成長來自於意識到：昨天的最佳解決方案，只是今天更好解決方案的墊腳石。
