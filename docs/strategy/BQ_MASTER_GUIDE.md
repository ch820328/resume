# BQ (Behavioral Questions) Master Guide: Google & Amazon Hybrid Strategy

This guide combines Google's official G/L (Googley & Leadership) principles, Amazon's Leadership Principles (LP), and senior interviewer experiences (Matt) for L4 Performance Infra candidates.

## 1. Core Mindset: From "Answering" to "Resonating"
The goal is to make the interviewer imagine that working with you (even at 10 PM on a Tuesday) is both productive and comfortable.

### A. Inner Monologue
*   **Concept**: Don't just tell "what happened"; tell "**what I was thinking at the time**."
*   **Technique**: Admit struggles or pressure (vulnerability) to make the story authentic.
*   **Example**: "When the system crashed right before deployment, I felt immense pressure. However, I realized that as the module owner, I had to stay calm to guide my teammates..."

### B. Future Pacing
*   **Concept**: Connect the ending to "**why this helps Google**."
*   **Technique**: Plant a seed in the interviewer's mind (Inception).
*   **Example**: "This persistence for performance data is something I will bring to Google's architecture optimization, ensuring every step is data-backed."

---

## 2. Story Structure: STAR Upgrade (Point A to Point B)
Use Amazon's timing to ensure high-quality output within 45 minutes.

| Phase | Time | Core Task |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S/T (Situation/Task)** | 1-2 mins | **Hook Them!** Quickly set a high-pressure or complex stage. |
| **Action** | 5-6 mins | **Details & DATA.** Use "I", focus on technical trade-offs. |
| **Result** | 1-2 mins | **Bring it Full Circle.** Quantifiable metrics and lessons learned. |

---

## 3. Four Key BQ Categories

### I. Ownership & Leadership
*   **Core**: Doing it for the company's long-term interest without being asked.
*   **Questions**:
    *   Share an experience where you went "outside your area" to solve a problem.
    *   How do you handle risks and communicate when a project might be delayed?

### II. Earn Trust & Disagree and Commit
*   **Core**: Persuading others with data without damaging relationships; committing even if you "lose" the argument.
*   **Questions**:
    *   Describe a time you disagreed with a manager or senior engineer on a technical decision.
    *   What direct feedback have you given a colleague, and what was the reaction?

### III. Dive Deep & High Standards
*   **Core**: Obsession with root causes. This is the soul of Performance Infra.
*   **Questions**:
    *   Share a case where you dug deep into technical details to find a hidden bug.
    *   Have you ever insisted on a higher standard when others thought "it was good enough"?

### IV. Learn and Be Curious
*   **Core**: Adaptability in ambiguous environments and with new technologies.
*   **Questions**:
    *   Share a decision you made with insufficient data/resources.
    *   What is the coolest technology you learned recently, and how did you apply it?

---

## 4. L4 Performance Infra Special Signals
*   **Performance Awareness**: Mention context switch costs, memory allocation/GC pressure.
*   **Automation Mindset**: "I didn't just fix it; I wrote a script to ensure it never happens again."
*   **Data-Driven**: Link decisions to A/B testing, Trace Logs, or Profiling data.

---

## 5. Execution Checklist
- [ ] **Clarify**: Confirm what trait the interviewer is looking for before answering.
- [ ] **Non-redundant**: Skip repetitive background; get to the Action quickly.
- [ ] **Consistent**: Ensure the story matches the persona shown in Coding rounds.
- [ ] **Practice "Why"**: For every action, ask "Why?" three times to find the deepest technical or business reason.
