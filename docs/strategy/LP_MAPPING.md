## Strategic Advice

1.  **Multiple Principles per Story**: Notice that one project (like the Jetson BSP Factory) can cover several principles. Be prepared to shift the focus depending on the question.
2.  **The "I" in Action**: When talking about these, emphasize: *"I developed..."*, *"I identified..."*, *"I chose..."*. Avoid saying "We" for your specific contributions.
3.  **Metrics are King**: Always mention the **90% reduction**, **5-minute vs 1-hour**, and **hundreds of nodes scanned in minutes**.
