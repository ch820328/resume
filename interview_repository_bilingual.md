# Bilingual Interview Question & Answer Repository (中英雙語面試題庫)

This repository contains interview questions, example answer strategies, and advice, mapped to **Google Leadership Principles**.
本題庫包含面試問題、答題策略與建議，並對應至 **Google 領導力原則**。

---

## 1. Core Google Readiness Principles (Google 核心準備原則)

| Principle (原則) | Definition (定義) | Interview Focus (面試重點) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Comfort with Ambiguity** (應對模糊性) | Making progress when requirements are unclear. | Show how you defined the path forward when others were stuck. (展示你在他人卡住時如何定義路徑。) |
| **Leadership without Authority** (無職權領導) | Influencing decisions and mentoring without a title. | Highlight being a "Force Multiplier" for the team. (強調成為團隊的「戰力倍增器」。) |
| **Bias for Action** (行動導向) | Proactively identifying and solving problems. | Show initiative beyond your assigned tasks (e.g., DXA improvements). (展示在分配任務之外的主動性。) |
| **Intellectual Humility** (謙遜與學習心態) | Openness to feedback and learning from mistakes. | Use the "Learning" part of STAR-L to show growth. (使用 STAR-L 的 Learning 部分展示成長。) |

## 2. Categorized Q&A (分類題庫)

### A. Leadership & Management (領導力與管理)
- **Q: Tell me about a time you led a project. (描述一次領導專案的經驗)**
    - **Google Context:** Focus on **Ownership** and **Risk Management**.
    - **English Advice:** Show how you identified a bottleneck early and communicated trade-offs to stakeholders.
    - **中文建議：** 展現你如何早期發現瓶頸，並向利害關係人溝通權衡 (Trade-offs)。
    - **Leadership Principle:** **Ownership** (所有權與責任感).

### B. Collaboration & Conflict (協作與衝突)
- **Q: Tell me about a disagreement with a partner. (描述一次與合作夥伴的分歧)**
    - **Google Context:** Focus on **Inclusive Collaboration** and **Data-Driven Resolution**.
    - **English Advice:** Never blame. Show how you used a POC or metrics to align on a technical direction.
    - **中文建議：** 絕不指責。展示你如何使用 POC (概念驗證) 或指標來達成技術方向的共識。
    - **Leadership Principle:** **Collaboration** (協作特質).

## 3. Detailed Breakdown by Source (各來源詳細解析 - 全雙語)

### 1. Big Interview - Leadership (領導力)
- **Q: Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership skills.** (分享展現領導能力的經驗。)
    - **Advice:** Focus on "Why" and "How". (強調「為什麼」與「如何做」。)
    - **Google Link:** Connects to **Leading without Authority**. (連結至「無職權領導力」。)
- **Q: Describe your leadership style.** (描述你的領導風格。)
    - **Advice:** Mention being adaptive and empowering. (提到具備適應力與賦能他人。)

### 2. Robert Half - Management (管理)
- **Q: How do you delegate tasks?** (你如何分配任務？)
    - **Advice:** Match tasks to individual strengths. (根據成員長處分配任務。)
    - **Google Link:** Shows you are a **Force Multiplier**. (展示你是團隊的「戰力倍增器」。)

### 3. The Interview Guys - SOAR Method (SOAR 方法)
- **Q: Lead through a difficult situation.** (帶領團隊度過困難。)
    - **Advice:** Highlight the **Obstacle** to show logic. (強調「障礙」以展示邏輯。)
- **Q: Influence without authority.** (無權限時如何影響他人？)
    - **Google Link:** Core for L4. Show how you earned trust through technical expertise. (L4 核心：展示你如何透過技術專業贏得信任。)

### 4. Big Interview - Teamwork (團隊合作)
- **Q: Input from different levels.** (跨層級溝通專案。)
    - **Advice:** Focus on communication styles for different stakeholders. (針對不同利害關係人調整溝通風格。)

### 5. Big Interview - Conflict Resolution (衝突解決)
- **Q: Disagreed with manager.** (與主管意見不合。)
    - **Advice:** Use data-driven discussion. (使用數據導向的討論。)

### 6. Workable - Conflict Management (衝突管理)
- **Q: Negative feedback from manager.** (主管給負面回饋時的應對。)
    - **Advice:** Treat feedback as a gift/growth opportunity. (將反饋視為成長機會。)
    - **Google Link:** **Intellectual Humility**. (連結至「謙遜與學習心態」。)

## 4. Summary Checklist for Success (成功檢查清單)

1. **No "Tier and Layer" (拒絕層級堆疊):** Are the steps logically linked? (步驟是否邏輯連貫？)
2. **Quantified Impact (量化影響):** Did you include a % or number in the Result? (結果是否有數據？)
3. **Google Readiness (Google 準備度):** Did you show comfort with ambiguity or leadership without authority? (是否展示了應對模糊性或無職權領導力？)
4. **Bilingual Practice (雙語練習):** Can you tell this story fluently in English using professional transition words? (是否能用專業轉折詞流暢地用英文講述故事？)
