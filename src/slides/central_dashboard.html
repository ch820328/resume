Unified Engineering Console

Platform Engineering & Full-Stack
Challenge:
  • Engineers suffered from high cognitive load managing fragmented CLIs (IPMI/Redfish) and disparate project boards
  • Constant context-switching between SSH, QEMU, and issue trackers severely degraded productivity
Solution:
  • Architected a Unified Go+Angular Portal aggregating cross-platform tickets and hardware protocols
  • Integrated WebSocket-based WebSSH, a WinSCP-style filesystem, and encapsulated QEMU environments
Impact:
  • Consolidated 10+ tools into a "Single Pane of Glass" with 1-click SUT hardware controls
  • Slashed environment setup time by 40% and enabled "1-Click Debugging", drastically lowering the validation barrier
