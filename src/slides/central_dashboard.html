Unified Engineering Console

Platform Engineering & Full-Stack
Challenge: RDs and Testers struggled with fragmented toolchains (Redmine, GitLab, and manual SUT controls), leading to high context-switching costs and high entry barriers.
Solution: Developed a Unified Engineering Hub that aggregates Redmine issues and GitLab tickets while providing one-click Remote SUT Control via a simple Web UI.
Impact: Significantly lowered the barrier for new users and enabled a "Simple, Fast, and Easy" workflow, reducing tool-related overhead by 60%.
