Unified Engineering Console

Platform Engineering & Full-Stack
Challenge: Engineering teams were siloed across dozens of disconnected internal tools, leading to fragmented data, duplicate effort, and massive overhead in cross-team visibility.
Solution: Developed a **Centralized Portal** that aggregates CI/CD status, infrastructure health, and project metrics via a unified **GraphQL API**. Built a responsive dashboard with real-time alerting.
Impact: Eliminated **50% of tool-switching overhead**. Improved incident response time by **30%** through unified alerting. Provided a "Single Source of Truth" for engineering leadership.
