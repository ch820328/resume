Data-Driven Governance: Issue Analytics

Quality Engineering & Metrics
Challenge:
  • Management struggled to identify at-risk projects, relying on misleading "gut feelings" and simple bug counts
  • Lacked objective metrics to track true engineering bottlenecks, such as stalled issue lifespans
Solution:
  • Developed a Data-Driven Governance Dashboard tracking issue "Aging" and "Processing Duration"
  • Implemented an Automated Forced Reminder Mechanism targeting stalled tickets
Impact:
  • Equipped leadership with objective metrics to preemptively rescue failing projects
  • Cultivated a culture of timely responsiveness, significantly reducing the lifespan of critical bugs
