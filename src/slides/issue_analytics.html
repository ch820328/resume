Engineering Governance Hub & Metric Analytics

Governance & Data Engineering
Challenge: [EN] Project health data was fragmented across multiple trackers (Redmine, Git logs). Manual KPI tracking was slow and lacked transparency for proactive risk management.
[TW] 專案健康度數據分散在多個追蹤系統（Redmine, Git 日誌）。手動 KPI 追蹤速度緩慢且缺乏透明度，難以進行主動的風險管理。
Solution: [EN] Engineered an Unattended Governance Hub with a Rule-based Risk Detection Engine and automated weekly audits scheduled via Laravel.
[TW] 架構了 無人值守治理中心 (Governance Hub)，具備 基於規則的風險偵測引擎，並透過 Laravel 實作了每週自動審計調度。
Impact: [EN] Established data-driven accountability, achieving a consistent 90% quality benchmark and reducing management overhead by 70%.
[TW] 建立了數據驅動的問責機制，達成持續性的 90% 質量指標，並將管理開銷降低了 70%。
