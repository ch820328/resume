Engineering Governance Hub & Metric Analytics

Governance & Data Engineering
Challenge: Project health data was fragmented across Redmine, Jira-style boards, and SVN/Git logs. Manual KPI tracking was slow, subjective, and lacked the transparency needed for proactive risk management.
Solution: Engineered an Unattended Governance Hub aggregating heterogeneous telemetry. Implemented a Rule-based Risk Detection Engine (e.g., B-1, V-3 tags) with automated 08:00 weekly audits scheduled via Laravel Console.
Impact: Established data-driven accountability across BIOS/Validation teams. Achieved a consistent 90% quality benchmark by identifying at-risk tasks with proactive algorithmic indicators, reducing management overhead by 70%.
