Industrial Orchestration & Automation Engine

Platform & Systems Engineering
Challenge: A team of 30+ engineers faced severe productivity losses due to hardware resource clashing during validation. Frequent system hangs required manual onsite resets, disrupting the 24/7 automation cycle.
Solution: Architected a Go-based Orchestration Engine featuring Redis-backed Distributed Locking and integrated Redfish/IPMI (SMCIPMITool) telemetry for fully automated Out-of-Band (OOB) recovery.
Impact: Eliminated 100% of hardware resource conflicts and achieved a truly Unattended Testing Cycle. Reduced hardware downtime by over 90% via automated self-healing for hung validation systems.
