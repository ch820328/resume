NVSSVT: Automation Orchestration Portal

Systems Engineering & Automation
Challenge:
  • Validating servers against NVIDIA compliance specs (NVSSVT) was a tedious, manual bottleneck
  • Complex configuration files were difficult for QA, causing inconsistent cross-team standards
Solution:
  • Architected a Go+Angular Orchestration Portal with a centralized, GUI-driven config editor
  • Implemented a robust job queuing system and real-time log streaming via WebSockets
Impact:
  • Enforced cross-team standardization and reduced manual QA intervention by 90%
  • Scaled validation throughput by 200%, ensuring servers meet NVIDIA specs faster
