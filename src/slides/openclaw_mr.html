OpenClaw: Multi-Agent AI Review Matrix

AI Orchestration & Orchestrated Intelligence
Challenge: Scaling high-quality code reviews for complex firmware and web services. Single-prompt LLM solutions suffered from context loss, hallucinations, and lacked multi-dimensional expert focus.
Solution: Architected a 3-Phase Matrix Pipeline: Master Orchestrator for complexity-based planning, Parallel Specialists for deep analysis (Security, Logic, Architecture), and a Consensus Aggregator for synthesis. Delivers structured improvement suggestions and critical feedback via a Blackboard Pattern.
Impact: Achieved 100% automated coverage of architectural gates. Expanded review coverage to include complex architectural consistency and cross-module logic analysis. Reduced human review cycle by 40% by delivering structured, high-fidelity feedback directly to GitLab MR threads.
