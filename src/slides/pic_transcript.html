Computer Vision & Data Digitization
Challenge: Digitizing thousands of legacy paper reports with complex layouts, tables, and varying image quality. Standard OCR tools failed to preserve structural relationships.
Solution: Built a custom pipeline using **OpenCV** for pre-processing (deskewing, noise reduction) and **Tesseract/EasyOCR** with a **Geometry-Aware Aggregator** to reconstruct document structure.
Impact: Achieved **98% accuracy** in table extraction. Automated the digitization of over **10,000+ records**, saving months of manual data entry labor.
