Firmware Consistency & Active Validation Framework

Firmware & Quality Engineering
Challenge: [EN] Hardware telemetry data often suffers from "Data Asymmetry" across Redfish and OS layers. Passive checks failed to detect propagation bugs during firmware updates.
[TW] 硬體遙測數據在 Redfish 和 OS 層之間經常存在「數據不對稱」。被動檢查無法偵測到韌體動態更新過程中的數據傳遞錯誤。
Solution: [EN] Engineered an Active Validation Framework featuring randomized data injection and CV-driven Interface Adaptation for 3-way reconciliation.
[TW] 開發了 主動驗證框架 (Active Validation)，透過隨機數據注入與 視覺驅動 (CV) 的介面適配，實現了三方數據的自動對帳與校驗。
Impact: [EN] Eliminated 100% of data propagation risks in manufacturing (L10/L11) and established a source-of-truth validation chain.
[TW] 徹底消除了 L10/L11 製造過程中的數據傳遞風險，建立了跨所有伺服器管理介面的「單一真相來源 (Source of Truth)」驗證鏈。
